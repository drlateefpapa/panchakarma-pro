Universal Classical Kerala Pottali Tying & Thermal Safety SOP

5-Step Cloth Folding & Tying Technique
  1. Lay 4 clean, unbleached cotton (Kora) squares (45 × 45 cm; open weave for Navarakizhi, medium weave for Ela/Podi/Kaadi Kizhi) flat.
  2. Place 1/4th of the warm prepared mixture neatly in the center of each square (target finished sphere: size of half a moderate coconut).
  3. Lift all 4 corners upward; fold 3 corners together neatly and wrap the 4th corner smoothly around them so the base is round and crease-free.
  4. Hold one end of a thick 35–45 cm cotton twine against the neck/stump with your left hand; wind the other end tightly upward in spiralling turns with your right hand.
  5. Tie both ends in a firm knot, leaving a compact vertical tuft (handle grip) at the top so therapists can hold and press evenly without slipping.
4-Bolus Rotation, Heat Check & Hygiene Rules
  • • 2 Active + 2 Warming: Keep 2 boluses warming in the pan/vessel on low flame while 2 therapists apply the other 2 synchronously on either side of the Droni.
  • • Mandatory Dorsum-of-Hand Test: Always test every reheated bolus on the dorsum (back) of the therapist’s own hand/wrist before touching the patient (safe target: 42–48 °C; lower for elderly, diabetic neuropathy, or Pitta Prakriti).
  • • Zero Cooling Gaps: Swap boluses every 2–3 minutes; cooling lag causes shivering (Vata aggravation). Work in a draft-free (Nivata) room.
  • • Daily Freshness & Single-Patient Rule: Prepare Navarakizhi, Mutta Kizhi, and Kaadi Kizhi fresh daily. Never reuse any pottali across different patients.
